Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are
processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Digital goods
are refundable only if they have not been downloaded. Subscription plans can be
cancelled at any time; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current
billing cycle, and no partial refunds are issued for unused time.

Security Note

All customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production
systems follows least-privilege and requires multi-factor authentication.
